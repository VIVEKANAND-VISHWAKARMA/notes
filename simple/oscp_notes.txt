--------------------------------------------------------------------------
LISTENERS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
nc -lvnp <PORT>              # plain netcat listener
rlwrap nc -lvnp <PORT>       # rlwrap adds readline (history/editing)
ncat -lvnp <PORT>            # ncat (Nmap project)
ncat --ssl -lvnp <PORT>      # ncat with TLS
socat -d -d TCP-LISTEN:<PORT> STDOUT

RLWRAP: small utility using GNU readline - gives editing keys + history.
NCAT : improved netcat from the Nmap project; adds SSL encryption.
SOCAT: connects two data sources (e.g. two hosts / a host and a file).
